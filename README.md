# Extent: State of Michigan
# Projection: WGS 1984
# Data Sources: Michigan GIS Open Data Portal - Sensitive Populations (https://gis-michigan.opendata.arcgis.com/datasets/e33ac11bf580479bb9b63f60f70a9982_0/explore?location=42.905474%2C-84.489451%2C11.19), Michigan GIS Open Data Portal - County (https://gis-michigan.opendata.arcgis.com/datasets/8fc9c089c11a4913b0768381fa07bf6d_0/explore), Michigan GIS Open Data Portal - Adjusted Census Urban Boundaries (https://gis-michigan.opendata.arcgis.com/datasets/d07b75149ae64a2da75ba9a01a4e11e6_5/explore). 
# Purpose: Display rates of low birth weights by census tract in order to visualize trends, for a general audience.
# Symbology: Darker colored polygons have higher rates or percentages of low birth weights. Lighter colored polygons have lower rates or percentages of low birth weights. Cities are marked by black and white circles and labeled with the city name. Urban areas are outlined with black lines, while county boundaries are outlined with grey lines. 
# Design: Chose red color family for data visualization due to its association with warnings, since darker reds in this visualization reflect lowered health in babies due to their low weights. 
# Limitations: Urban areas are outlined, but the individual city borders are not due to the data used.
